# Product Knowledge: One-source signage, fabrication and branding solution (design, fabricate, install)

## What We Sell
The Branding Lab is a full-service branding and fabrication agency in Trichardt, Mpumalanga, South Africa that designs, manufactures and installs every branded surface a business needs, from illuminated 3D storefront signage and SANS 1186-compliant safety signs to vehicle fleet wraps, large-format print and corporate identity, with a marketing division covering web, photo, video and drone content. It is built for B2B clients such as automotive dealerships, franchises, retail chains, mines, industrial plants and fleet operators that want one vendor to manage the full physical brand rollout instead of coordinating a designer, printer, welder and installer separately.

## Core Value Proposition
A one-source solution that designs, fabricates and installs every branded surface a business needs, so clients deal with a single vendor instead of a designer, printer, welder and installer.

## Key Benefits
- Deal with one vendor for design, fabrication and installation instead of coordinating multiple suppliers
- See photo-realistic mockups on your actual building or vehicle before any metal is cut, reducing risk and rework
- Consistent quality control because cutting, printing, welding and wiring all happen in-house
- Signage built to comply with national corporate brand guidelines and SANS 1186 safety regulations
- Ongoing aftercare (maintenance, cleaning, re-skins) so branded assets stay current through seasonal campaigns
- Unified brand aesthetic across every sign, vehicle, uniform and social post, from the street to the screen
